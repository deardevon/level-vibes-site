How it works

Three taps
1

Tap your energy

Running low? Level 1. Some energy? Level 2. Full tank? Level 3.

2

It picks one thing

From your chores, meals or any list you make. Not feeling it? Pick again, or “Not today”.

3

Let’s go

Start with just 2 minutes if you like. Watch the ring close, and that’s a win.

What it does

Small on purpose

Every part is there to make the next thing easier to start.

Getting started

Just 2 minutes

A small start that still counts, and a tiny first step for anything on your list.

Timers

Watch the ring close

Set a time for each level. It counts down on your Lock Screen, rings when time’s up, and you get a win when the ring closes.

Lists

A list for anything

Chores and meals to start, ready-made lists for exercise, self-care and more, or your own.

Picking

Really random

Like shuffling a deck: everything in a level comes up once before anything repeats.

Wins

Little wins count

Your week at a glance and achievements for showing up. No streaks to break.

Everywhere

Wrist and widgets

Pick a level on Apple Watch, from your Home Screen, or with “Surprise me” in Control Center.

Together

Share with your household

Share lists with the people you live with. Once someone does a task, it’s off everyone’s list for the day.

Focus

Sounds and music

Work along to rain, brown noise or waves, or link a playlist to a list.

Private

Yours, and only yours

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your lists stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Nudges

A gentle daily nudge

Turn on a reminder that says something different each day, and start straight from it.
