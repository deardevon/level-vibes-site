Some days are Level 1 days

Some days you can deep-clean the kitchen. Some days a bowl of cereal counts as dinner. Tell Level Vibes how much you’ve got, and it chooses something that fits. Not feeling it? Pick again, or set it aside with “Not today”.

Everything it does

Small on purpose. Every part is there to make the next thing easier to start.

A list for anything

Chores and meals to start, plus ready-made lists for exercise, self-care, admin, studying, social plans and projects. Or write your own.

Really random

Like shuffling a deck: everything in a level comes up once before anything repeats.

Not today

Set a pick aside until tomorrow. One-time things can be removed once they’re done.

On your home screen

Tap a level right from the Pick a Level widget. Timers count down on your lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Share the vibe

Turn today’s pick into a picture for your Story, in your theme’s colors.

Yours, and private

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your lists stay on your phone. Back them up to Notes or email any time.
