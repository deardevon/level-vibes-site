Privacy Policy

Level Vibes · Last updated 24 September 2026

The short version: Everything you put in Level Vibes stays on your phone. We don’t collect your lists, your name or anything about you. The one thing the app sends anywhere is a random installation number when it checks for app updates, and that isn’t linked to you.

What stays on your device

Your lists, the options in them, your history, timers and settings (like your theme) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. We can’t see them.

App updates

Level Vibes can receive small fixes and improvements without a full App Store update. To do that, it checks our update service (run by Expo, the company whose tools Level Vibes is built with) when it opens. Each check sends:

  • a random installation number, created when the app is installed and not linked to your name, Apple ID or anything else about you,
  • the app version and the kind of device (iPhone or iPad).

This is used only to deliver the right update and to count how many installations are using the app. It isn’t used for advertising or tracking, and it contains none of your lists or history. Deleting the app deletes the installation number. See Expo’s privacy policy.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts or sign-ups.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Backups you export

When you use “Export”, Level Vibes creates a block of text with your lists and hands it to your phone’s share sheet. Where it goes next — Notes, Mail, a message to a friend — is your choice, and it’s handled by that app, not by Level Vibes.

Purchases

The Plus Pack is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple whether the Plus Pack has been bought, so it can unlock it.

The web version

The web version of Level Vibes is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most websites, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Level Vibes itself doesn’t collect anything.

Children

Level Vibes is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
