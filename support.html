How does it choose?

Like shuffling a deck of cards: within each level, every option comes up once, in random order, before anything repeats. You’ll never get the same thing twice in a row.

How do I add or change options?

Tap “Edit” under the level buttons. Put one option per line, and give each level a short description. Tap “+ New list” at the top to make a new list.

How do I back up my lists or move them to a new phone?

Tap “Backup/Restore Lists”, then “Export all lists”, and save the text somewhere safe — Notes or an email to yourself works well. On the new phone, open the same section, paste the whole thing into “Restore”, and choose “Replace all my lists”.

I deleted the app. Are my lists gone?

Lists are saved only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes them. If you exported a backup, you can restore it. Export now and then to stay safe.

What’s in the Plus Pack?

A one-time unlock with more than three lists, color themes, history with weekly totals and streaks, timers for each level, and alternate app icons. Level Vibes stays free without it.

I bought the Plus Pack. How do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open the Plus Pack and tap “Restore purchase”, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. There’s no extra charge.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How does the timer let me know when time’s up?

In the App Store version, you get a notification with a sound, even if the app is closed or your phone is locked. The first time you start a timer, Level Vibes asks for permission to send notifications — if you said no, you can turn them on in Settings › Notifications › Level Vibes.

In the web version, iPhone doesn’t allow alerts from web apps, so it plays a chime if the app is open when time runs out (not when your phone is on silent). Either way, the timer keeps counting while the app is closed.
